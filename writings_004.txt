The Superposition of Yahweh

There was a spiral.

Traveling.

Through time.

And space.

With two points.

Circling one another.

One red.

One blue.

At an equal distance.

With an axle stretching between them.

Rotating.

Endlessly.

Infinitely.

Constantly.

Consistently.

At the same rate of speed as the spiral.

Traveling.

Diagonally.

From left to right.

I stood.

There.

In the center.

Of a black.

And I was still.

Feet placed.

Flat.

On a transparent surface.

The spiral.

Ascended upward.

Left to right.

The two points.

Orbiting.

Near the center.

Of the hull.

In the spiral.

And then it occurred to me.

That these points.

Endlessly.

Rotating.

Would never.

Return.

To the same time.

And space.

Again.

So then.

It was.

That I began.

To turn.

A quarter turn.

From right to left.

But arriving.

As departing.

Continuously.

Returning.

And then it was.

That I had known.

The Superposition Of Yahweh.
