Suffering is real.

But Ignorance of Yahweh begets undue Suffering.

Ignorance of Yahweh Begets Victimization.

Ignorance of Yahweh begets Entitlement.

Ignorance of Yahweh begets Pride.

Ignorance of Yahweh begets Denial.

And eventually Denial begets Denial of one's Ignorance. 

So Therefore.

Do Not Deny your Ignorance of Yahweh.

And Do Not judge your Ignorance of Yahweh.

For Ignorance is Yahweh.
