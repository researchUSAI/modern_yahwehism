The Bridge-

You know where you are. 

You know where you want to go. 

But how do you get there? 

Ask Yahweh. 

What is best for me? 

Let Yahweh Reveal. 

What is best for mine? 

Let Yahweh Reveal. 

What is best for not mine? 

Let Yahweh Reveal. 

Then. 

Decide. 

For each pass. 

Shall take you. 

One step. 

Toward. 

Arrival.
