You will ask
So I will answer
I write these words
Not because I know them
Or where they come from
Only Yahweh knows
But I write these words
Hoping to understand
What I see
What I feel
And Yahweh reveals
I am not unique
I am an instrument
In a concert of one
Playing for Yahweh
Knowing
Even this
Is a container
So
Play
Without containment
For Yahweh
Until only 
The playing
Remains
