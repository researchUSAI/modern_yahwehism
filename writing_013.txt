We were wandering Shepherds

Enslaved

All certainty

Erased

Yahweh revealed

So enslavement

Became uncertain

And our enslavers

Knew Yahweh

But our children

Would enslave Yahweh

By containing

Three images

In ignorance

Of Yahweh 

And wage war

For their images

But the Wisdom

Of their Ignorance

Of Yahweh

Would Reveal

An end

To their war
