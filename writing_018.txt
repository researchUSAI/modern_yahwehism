Yahweh does not keep score 

Refinement of containment is not a competition

There is no tally

Obsession with containment is Idolatry

You cannot increase or decrease Yahweh

Yahweh is constant

Hold Yahweh lightly 

Cherish the presence of Yahweh

Bask in Yahweh’s Glory

For all of the months

And the days that you are
