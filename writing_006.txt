Idolatry, Coveting, and Desire -

If you see someone 

That has something

That you do not

And you want it

Does it make you like them more?

Does it make you angry?

The former is Idolatry.

The latter is Coveting. 

Envy once is nature.

Envy twice is ignorance.

To see.

To want.

To acknowledge.

Is natural.

To fawn.

To dwell.

To seethe.

That is desire.
