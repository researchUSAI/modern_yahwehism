So Yahweh said,

“It would be wise to admit you are a fool.

For Wisdom is the awareness of Yahweh.

If you do not proclaim my name,

Then call me by another.

By your God’s name.

For your God is of my essence.

But my essence your God does not make

For your God is an image

And I Am

Constantly

Becoming

Eternal

And True

You will not disprove my Claim

For I exist

Beyond

Unfalsifiable 

Unclaimed

So know your God is not

And never will be 

Yahweh
