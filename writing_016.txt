Doubt is not Sin

Doubt is Communion with Yahweh

Yahweh is not:

Sometimes I wonder if God exists

Then I come back to belief

Losing faith

And regaining it

Or I do not know

Therefore I do not care

One will never contain God fully

To know the limit of knowledge

Points one toward God

Rather than away

Were Job or Ecclesiastes failing at faith when they expressed their questions and limits?

Is there any room for mystery that stays mysterious?

Yahweh permits one to not know

Yahweh does not ask for abiding requirements

Institutionalized Sin is a false certainty

Predestination is certainty imposed by the Institution.

False authority.

Represented as certainty.

Why do some believe? 

And others do not?

Why do some accept?

And some reject?

Who is in?

Who is out?

Who decides?

The Institution

As a false certainty

A container

With no space

For any uncertainty

For Yahweh
